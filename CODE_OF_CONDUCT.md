# Code of conduct

Pyrite is a personal project with a small corner of GitHub: its issues, pull requests and Discussions. I want anyone to be able to ask a question or point out a flaw there without being made to feel small for it.

## What I ask

- Be specific and kind. Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, especially from someone new to Python, Git or Claude Code.
- Keep other people's data out, the same way [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) asks you to keep out your own: no one else's resume, mail, contact details or anything that identifies them.

## What isn't welcome

- Harassment, insults, slurs, and sexual language or images.
- Personal attacks, including about someone's job search, work history or layoff.
- Posting anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, and promotion that has nothing to do with the thread.

## Reporting

Open the **…** menu on the comment, issue or discussion, choose **Report content**, then **Report to repository admins**. Only I see that report. For something GitHub itself should act on, such as threats or spam accounts, choose **Report abuse** in the same place.

## What I'll do

I read every report. I may edit, hide or delete content, lock a thread, or block an account from the repository, and I'll usually say why in the thread unless that would make things worse. It's one person, so I'll act as soon as I can, without a promised timeline.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and Discussions, and anywhere someone speaks for the project. For anything it doesn't cover, the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) is the guide.
