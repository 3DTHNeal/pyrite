### Fixed

- **Possible reply** no longer flags mail from an employer's shop, such as an order or a delivery. It skips a "Thank you for your interest" message whose text says neither a rejection nor an interview, which only the Gmail connector can read. A status update that names another job at the same company, such as one already closed, no longer goes on every open application there.
- The field report's "since then you passed on" no longer counts a pass you undid.
- The dashboard loads about three times faster with a few hundred jobs. Gaps to answer read the fact bank once rather than once per gap, and each posting's pay and deadline are read once while its text stays the same.
