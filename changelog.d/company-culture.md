### Added

- **Employer research** on each job's panel: **Research with Claude** has Claude search the web for how the employer works and treats people. It covers how work gets done (small steps or phase gates), how performance is reviewed (forced curves, quotas of low ratings, performance-based layoffs, rehire bans), layoffs, the labor and legal record, and what employees say. Every point links its source, held to the industry outlook's source check, and the panel links Glassdoor, Indeed, Blind, Comparably, Violation Tracker and NLRB searches for you to read yourself. Every job at the employer shares the report, and tailoring sums it up under `## Culture` in the job's notes. `pyrite culture <id>` does the same from the command line, and `model` under `[culture]` picks the model.
