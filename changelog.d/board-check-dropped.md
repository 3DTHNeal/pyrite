### Added

- The status line's details say how many of the postings the boards list your filters leave out, and why: by title, by location, below the score floor, not openings, with a scam's tells or past their deadline. "0 new" alone read the same on a quiet day as when every title missed your role types. The numbers start with the next board check.
- The Sources tab says the same for each board, and in plain text rather than grey when a board's postings are all left out.
- While a search has never kept a posting from a board, the to-do list says so under **Finish setup**, with why they're left out, and opens Roles you want or Where you'll work.
- After the first pan, `pyrite discover` also says what your filters leave out in all, and its JSON has it as `left_out`. What it counted as filtered out was only what it met for the first time.
