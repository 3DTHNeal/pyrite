### Added

- **Companies to watch** on the Profile tab edits the job boards Pyrite reads, the companies you check by hand and the Workday searches. Paste a careers page address and it works out the Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or Workday board from it. **Test** reads one board once, as `pyrite sources` does, and says whether it answers and how many postings it lists. Each board is changed on its own line in `search.toml`, so your comments stay.
