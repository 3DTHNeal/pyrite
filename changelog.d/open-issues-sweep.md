### Changed

- The **Pipeline** column for jobs you've sent is now **Waiting to hear**, as the rail calls it, with *Applied, nothing back yet* under it.
- The **Closed** tab shows when each job closed and the note you gave when it did, under **Note**, in place of the fit reason. A pass with no note falls back to its fit reason. The tab has a search box and a filter by outcome.
- **Fit and interview notes** in the job panel show their headings, lists, tables, bold and links as such, not as Markdown. Commands in help text, such as `pyrite inbox`, show as code.
- The status popover names each failing source and what went wrong, up to three, with **Open Sources** to see them all.
- **Prospects** says when a remembered filter is leaving postings out, such as *1 of 4 postings, filtered by track*, outlines that filter, and offers **Show all**.
- The demo is consistent: events are dated when they happened, so the field report isn't out of date on opening; both **Ready to send** jobs have built documents that pass the assay; the scorer scores every posting; mail reads as working; and every button that would start a Claude run is disabled up front.

### Fixed

- A job you've applied to is no longer told to rebuild its documents or rewrite its resume, even with no copy of what was sent on record. Which drafter built them and the resume's share of the posting's skill terms stay, as facts.
