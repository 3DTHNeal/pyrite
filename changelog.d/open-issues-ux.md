### Added

- **Go to**, in the top bar or with Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac), finds a job, a view, a section of Profile or Settings, or an action by a few letters of its name, and goes there.
- **J** and **K** move down and up To do, the Pipeline board, Prospects and Closed when no job panel is open, and **Enter** opens the one you're on.

### Changed

- **To do** groups items of one kind under one heading that says what they share, so each line keeps only its own words. A group folds into one line naming its companies, a group of more than three starts folded, and the browser remembers which you've folded.
